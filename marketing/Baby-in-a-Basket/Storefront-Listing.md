# Baby in a Basket

A fictional printable genealogy mystery.

Mabel grew up with the story of a basket left in a church pew. The names of her birth parents never came with it. Follow census entries, compare birth records, and trace household connections to reconstruct her beginnings.

Investigate solo or together using the supplied records. Optional hints help you continue; a separate sealed solution lets you check your reasoning when you are ready.
