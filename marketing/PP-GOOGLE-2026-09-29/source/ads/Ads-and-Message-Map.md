Prepared 2026-09-29 for **porchpress.store**. **Ads prepared only; landing page published; $39.99 USD cart and checkout verified.** No Google account objects were staged and no Google ad spend was made. Remaining launch gates are listed below.

## The agreed offer and buyer

**Four Case Files. One Collection. — $39.99 USD**

Baby in a Basket, The Inheritance, Roscoe’s Grave, and The Wrong Widow. Fictional investigations through family records, delivered as printable PDFs. Nothing ships. Play solo or together. Every case includes a player packet, progressive hints, a separate sealed solution and START-HERE print notes.

Primary buyer: an experienced case-file player seeking another investigation, willing to examine historical family records and print digital files. Age, gender and income are unverified. This collection is not being advertised as four murder cases.

The $39.99 commercial choice is approved and uses **four existing products in one cart**, one of each case. A fixed $11.97 discount (`PORCHFOUR3999`) reduces the $51.96 subtotal to **$39.99 USD**. The 2026-09-29 cart check confirmed four non-shipping items, and the checkout GET check showed all four titles, the price and the discount. No new bundle product or variant is needed. Existing download fields are configured for all four products; customer receipt and successful access to all downloads remain unverified.

## Keyword candidates and exclusions

Eight seeds are documented in `keyword-candidates.csv`. Seven can be researched for a tightly qualified test; **cold case game printable** is explicitly held because police/murder expectations can conflict with this family-record collection. No seed is a validated live keyword yet.

Exact and phrase match can still reach meaning-based variants. Current U.S. Keyword Planner volume, competition and bid estimates are absent. Blank CSV cells and JSON nulls mean **unverified**, not zero. Match labels do not replace actual search-term review.

42 narrow phrase-negative candidates cover free products, video-game platforms, jobs, hosted parties, real-record research and existing-game answer searches. They are proposed rules, not observations of actual traffic. Do not blanket-negate **family**, **genealogy**, **records**, **solution**, **PDF**, **download**, **online**, or phone/device words: some can describe the intended buyer or included product.
