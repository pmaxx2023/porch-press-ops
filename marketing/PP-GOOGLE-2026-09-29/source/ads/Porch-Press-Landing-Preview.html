Before you open the file

A few useful answers.

Is this a physical product?

No. The collection is four digital PDF cases to download and print. Nothing ships.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. These cases can be played solo or with a small group. Choose a case, open the player packet, and follow its START-HERE notes.

What kind of mystery is this?

A playable investigation built around fictional family records. You compare names, dates, relationships, and competing claims to reach a conclusion supported by the evidence.

Are the cases based on real families?

No. The characters and evidence are fictional. The investigations use family records and competing claims as the material for a playable mystery.

What if I get stuck?

Each case includes progressive hints so you can seek help, then continue investigating. Keep the sealed solution separate until you are ready to check your conclusion.

What is included for $39.99?

Baby in a Basket, The Inheritance, Roscoe’s Grave, and The Wrong Widow. Each includes a player packet, progressive hints, a sealed solution, and START-HERE print notes.
