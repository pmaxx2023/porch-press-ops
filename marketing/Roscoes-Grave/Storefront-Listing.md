**Who is really buried here?**

A daughter asks a mason to add her mother's name to a familiar headstone. The cemetery book says someone else is buried there.

Follow the paper trail from stone order to burial ledger, funeral account and family register. Resolve the names, reconstruct the relationships, and decide what the memorial should say.

Roscoe's Grave is a fictional genealogy investigation about the stories families preserve and the records they leave behind.
