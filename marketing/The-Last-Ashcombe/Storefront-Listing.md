# The Last Ashcombe

**Who inherits the house? A printable genealogy and succession mystery.**

Julian Ashcombe is dead. Three relatives have asked for the keys.

One has the family name. Another claims the oldest branch. A third wants the trustees to read the settlement before deciding anything.

You have the records. You must prove who inherits.

## The investigation

Step into a fictional country-estate dispute in 1913. Read a family settlement, reconstruct the relevant branches, compare birth and death entries, follow changing surnames, and test the claims against the rules.

A familiar name is a lead. A convincing story is a claim. Your final answer needs evidence.

The Last Ashcombe is a self-contained printable investigation for solo play or shared play around the same records. No host or outside research is required. All succession rules are provided, so no legal or genealogy expertise is assumed.
