## Full product description

**The Wrong Widow** drops you into an open pension fight in southeastern Indiana.

Two women have filed Civil War widow’s pension claims for the same enlisted man. The bureau papers already contradict each other. Marriages, musters, censuses, death papers, and affidavits appear to back **both** sides. The records disagree the way witnesses disagree.

Your job is not to decode a cipher. Your job is to adjudicate:

1. which claim is supportable from the paper  
2. what each woman asserted  
3. what the households and the soldier’s trail actually show when you stop treating any single page as omniscient truth  

When you finish, the case resolves. Progressive hints stay sealed until you need them. The solution packet stays sealed until you submit.
