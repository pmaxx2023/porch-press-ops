# The Inheritance

**A printable genealogy mystery. Three claims. One rightful heir.**

Henry Vale has died without naming an heir. His will leaves the estate to his sister's eldest surviving child.

Three people claim it. One has the earliest birth date. One carries the family surname. One uses a name that does not appear in the baptism register.

Your job is to follow the records and tell the executor who should receive the keys.

Compare census extracts, parish registers, a school admission, a death notice and a burial index. Reconstruct the family. Distinguish a shared name from a shared identity. Every conclusion must earn its place in your final report.

Play alone or cooperate with a small group. Read the commission, choose your next record, compare its details, revise your theory and commit your conclusion. No host or outside research is required.
