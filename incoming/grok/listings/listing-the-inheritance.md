## Full product description
**The Inheritance** drops you into Ruth Fenwick’s office with the mill keys on the desk and a will that will not settle itself.

Three claimants arrive with packets. The document requires a particular relationship and an order of birth. Your job is to weigh what the papers support — and what they do not — without treating any one story as gospel.

When you finish, the case resolves. Progressive hints stay sealed until you need them. The solution stays sealed until you commit.
