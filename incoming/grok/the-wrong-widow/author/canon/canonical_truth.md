# The Wrong Widow — Canonical Truth (internal)

Dataset: `wrong-widow-canon`  
Setting: Ripley + Jefferson counties, SE Indiana. Player frame ~1890–1892.

## One-sentence truth

Private **Elias M. Corbett** (Co. C, 36th Indiana Infantry) deserted after a Nashville hospital stretch that left contradictory “dead” / “deserted” paper; his first marriage to **Martha Jane Ashby** was never dissolved; he later married **Lydia Ann Briggs** as a supposed widower; when he died in 1887, both women filed sincere-looking widow claims — but only Martha’s undissolved prior marriage is **supportable**.
