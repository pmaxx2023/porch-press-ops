## Top release blockers

Regardless of overall progress, do not release with any of these open:

1. protected answer visible in any public source or client payload;
2. required clue or recovery route missing;
3. non-unique factual reconstruction;
4. premature cast knowledge or capstone access;
5. reality boundary likely to imply a real active disappearance;
6. inaccessible required evidence;
7. broken customer entry, state persistence, or finale;
8. current legacy product/assets no longer preserved;
9. unapproved live change, spend, domain, or recurring service.
