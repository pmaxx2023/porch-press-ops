Claim boundary
The page reports what the later label says and explicitly withholds archive verification of the woman's identity.

Catalog note

The print shows a woman at Bellwether's 1951 winter gathering. A blue-pencil note on the reverse reads ‘Eleanor Hart — back for the winter benefit.’ Bellwether's 1948 chronology reports Hart missing three years earlier. The label is retained as a record claim, not a verified identification. The uncropped contact sheet is cataloged separately.
