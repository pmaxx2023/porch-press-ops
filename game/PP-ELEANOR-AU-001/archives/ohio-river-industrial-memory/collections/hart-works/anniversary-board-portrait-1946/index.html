Catalog note

Formal board-room portrait made for the works' fiftieth-anniversary circular. The typewritten identification on the reverse and the separately printed employee circular agree on the sitter order. This is the latest verified image of Eleanor Hart in the collection.

Four Hart Works board members pose around a table in a black-and-white 1946 studio photograph; the record includes a separately viewable typed reverse caption.

Media brief: Black-and-white 8×10 board-room portrait. Eleanor is 59, seated third from left, with an angular jaw, close-set eyes, silver hair waved above the ears, half-lens spectacles in her right hand, and a small crescent scar at the left temple. Preserve enough resolution for facial comparison. Produce a separately framed reverse image with the typewritten caption and studio stamp.

Transcription

Reverse, typewritten: ‘Hart Works — Fifty Years. Board room, 2 May 1946. Left to right: Robert S. Hart, managing president; Henry C. Hart, purchasing; Eleanor M. Hart, records and Byrne trust papers; Lillian Morey, secretary.’ Studio stamp: ‘Voss Commercial, neg. 46-118.’
