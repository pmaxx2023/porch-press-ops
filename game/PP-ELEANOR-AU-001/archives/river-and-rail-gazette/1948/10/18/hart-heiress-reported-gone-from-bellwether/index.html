Transcription

HART HEIRESS REPORTED GONE FROM BELLWETHER HOUSE

Miss Eleanor Mae Hart, 62, records trustee for the Hart Works interests, was reported gone yesterday after a woman in Miss Hart's navy coat left Bellwether House shortly before six o'clock. A reservation issued in Hart's name was used aboard the Midland & Western evening train for Chicago.

A house employee said the departing passenger wore a close veil and entered a hired motor at the service drive. A railway porter, reached after midnight, confirmed serving a passenger traveling on the Hart reservation.

Henry Hart received a telegram bearing his sister's initial: ‘I have gone where the Hart name cannot reach me. Do not bring me back. E.’

Robert S. Hart, managing president of Hart Works, said the family had no reason to suspect violence and believed his aunt had left voluntarily after strain connected with family trust records. Bellwether director Ada Pike declined to identify residents or discuss the house's internal register. No witness quoted by the Gazette said that he or she saw Miss Hart's uncovered face during the departure.
