Transcription

17 June — Mr. Kessler remembers Francis being called Frank Byrne after Ellen took rooms near the river. James says the names make it impossible. They make it necessary. A name ought not to be a thing a family can take away. Find Ellen's marriage line before James finds the old ledger.
