Catalog note

This research file summarizes a closed historical reconstruction. On 18 June 1912, Eleanor Hart was confined in the works' lower pattern store by her brother James Hart after she found records establishing that Frank Byrne was their father's eldest surviving son, Francis Hart. James sent a telegram intended to make Eleanor's absence appear voluntary. Eleanor was found the following morning, and the records she assembled restored Frank's place in the family and works history. The file is retained here because the language and method of that first disappearance recur in later Hart collections.

Transcription

CLOSED RESEARCH SUMMARY — Eleanor Mae Hart, born 4 October 1886. Missing interval: evening 18 June to morning 19 June 1912. Location recovered: lower pattern store, Hart Tool & Pattern Works. Principal false record: telegram presented as Eleanor's voluntary departure. Record outcome: Francis Hart, raised as Frank Byrne, identified as the son of Samuel Hart and Ellen Byrne Hart. Responsible party: James Hart. No claim in this summary concerns Eleanor's later life.
